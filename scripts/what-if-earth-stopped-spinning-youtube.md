## Описание

Right now, you're moving at 1,280 km/h with the Earth. What if it stopped for just one second?

The ground stops, but the air keeps going. The wind gets stronger than any hurricane ever recorded, glass blows out of skyscrapers, and then the Earth starts spinning again, all at once.

Physics is simplified for the animation.
