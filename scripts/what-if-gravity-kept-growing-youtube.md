What if gravity kept getting stronger? 🌍 #shorts

## Описание

Right now, Earth pulls you down with 1 g. What if that number kept going up?

At 5 g you can't stay standing. At 12 g most buildings give way, then bridges, then the mountains. The air gets squeezed into a layer thinner than a skyscraper, the sky turns black, the Moon falls and is torn apart, and at about 718 million g, Earth becomes a black hole.

Physics is simplified for the animation.
