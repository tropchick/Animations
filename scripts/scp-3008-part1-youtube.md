## Описание

You walk into a furniture store to buy a $19.99 lamp. When you turn around, the entrance is gone.

This is Log 01 of Ben, who got lost in SCP-3008, a store with no end. At 10 p.m. the lights go out, and the staff come out.
Rule one: when the lights go out, hide.
